Template noticeThis policy describes the intended practices for launch. Before going live, have it reviewed by a qualified professional against the services you actually use (hosting, analytics, email provider) and the laws that apply to your readers, such as the GDPR, UK GDPR and U.S. state privacy laws.

Summary

  • No advertising trackers, no third-party advertising cookies, and no selling of personal data.
  • Newsletter emails don't contain tracking pixels or link-tracking redirects.
  • We collect only what's needed to run the site, send the newsletter you asked for, and reply to you.

Information we collect

When you browse

Our hosting provider records standard server logs (IP address, browser type, page requested, time) for security and reliability. These logs are kept for a short period and then deleted. If we use analytics, we use a privacy-focused, cookieless service that doesn't track you across sites, and we report only aggregate figures.

The site stores a single preference in your browser if you choose a light or dark theme. It's never sent to us.

When you subscribe to the newsletter

We collect your email address and the date you subscribed. We use it only to send the Hardenwire Briefing. You can unsubscribe at any time from the link in every email, and your address is then removed from our active list.

When you contact us

We receive the name, email address and message you send. We use them to reply and, for tips and corrections, to follow up. We don't publish your name without permission.

Sharing

We share data only with service providers who help run the site and newsletter (for example, hosting and email delivery), under contracts that limit their use of it. We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising.

Third-party content

Pages load fonts from Google Fonts. Links to external sites, such as official resources, are provided for convenience. Those sites have their own privacy practices.

Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or export your personal data, and to object to certain processing. To use them, contact us through the contact page. We'll respond within the time required by applicable law.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS, access to our systems is protected with strong authentication, and we keep the amount of data we hold to a minimum.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, we'll update the date above and, for significant changes, notify newsletter subscribers.
