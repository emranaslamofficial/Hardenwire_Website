How to report an error

Use the contact form and choose "Correction or update request", or email the corrections address listed there. Please include the article's URL, the text you believe is wrong, and, if you can, a source. We review every request.

What happens next

  • Factual errors (a wrong setting name, an inaccurate statistic, incorrect advice) are corrected as soon as they're verified. A correction note is added at the end of the article stating what was changed and when.
  • Significant errors that could have led a reader to take a harmful action or a weaker security measure are noted at the top of the article, as well as at the end.
  • Outdated information caused by a software change, such as a menu that has moved, is handled as an update. We revise the guide and change its Updated date, without a correction note unless the original was wrong when published.
  • Typos and minor wording changes are fixed without a note.

What we don't do

We don't remove or rewrite articles to hide errors, and we don't change articles at the request of companies unless the request identifies a factual inaccuracy.

Response times

We aim to acknowledge correction requests within two working days, and to resolve verified errors that affect security advice within one working day of confirming them.
