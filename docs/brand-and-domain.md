# Brand & Domain Research

## Method

Domain availability was checked against the **Verisign RDAP service**
(`https://rdap.verisign.com/com/v1/domain/<name>.com`). Verisign is the
registry operator for `.com`, so this is the authoritative source for whether a
`.com` exists in the registry:

- HTTP `200` → the domain **is registered** (taken)
- HTTP `404` → **no registration record** (available to register at that moment)

Checks were run on **18 November 2026**. About 150 names were tested in five
batches. The shortlist was checked a second time just before writing this
document.

> **Caveats.** A 404 means the name is not registered. It does not guarantee
> standard pricing: some registrars list unregistered names as "premium". Also,
> a domain can be registered by anyone at any time. Register your pick soon,
> and run a trademark search (USPTO TESS / EUIPO / WIPO Global Brand Database)
> before you invest in the brand. **Nothing was purchased.**

| **Tagline** | *Security and privacy, explained plainly.* |

### Why Hardenwire works

- **"Harden"** is real security vocabulary. *Hardening* a system means
  reducing its attack surface. It also works as plain English ("harden your
  accounts"), so it suits both expert and beginner readers.
- **"Wire"** places the name in the tradition of news and publishing (a news
  *wire*, *wire* service) and suggests networks and connectivity. The brand
  sounds like a publication, not a product.
- It covers **both cybersecurity and privacy**. "Hardening" your digital life
  includes locking down data and tracking as well as devices.
- Ten letters made of two common English words, no ambiguous spelling, no hyphens or
  numbers. It's easy to say on a podcast and easy to type from memory.
- It doesn't sound like an AI startup (no "-ify", "-ly", "AI", or "GPT") and
  doesn't play on hacker clichés.
- It makes a strong wordmark. The H-W initials allow a simple monogram (used as
  the favicon), and the name leaves room for sub-brands: *Hardenwire Briefing*
  (newsletter), *Hardenwire Guides*, *Hardenwire Labs*.
